explanation: A question that asks for a factual explanation, identification, or clarification about a fact, system, institution, or observable phenomenon, rather than asking what the author should do or how people should judge it.
practical_guidance: A question that asks for a method, recommendation, or action to solve a concrete practical or technical problem.
personal_advice: A question about the author's own emotional, social, or interpersonal situation that asks what they should do, say, or change.
social_judgment: A question that asks whether a behavior, belief, or social norm is common, acceptable, justified, or preferable in a broader social sense.
